How this works

Decision tree on the Portuguese student performance dataset

What it predicts

The model estimates whether a student passes or fails based on demographics, family background, habits, and support factors — not on final exam scores shown in the form.

Model

Categorical answers are one-hot encoded; a decision tree with entropy splits and maximum depth 5 is trained on the first 500 shuffled rows and evaluated on the rest, with 5-fold cross-validation reported at startup.

Using the form

Adjust fields or tap a sample profile, then Predict. Probabilities show how confidently the tree leans toward pass or fail for that profile.
